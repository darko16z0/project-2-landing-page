Classic Grilled Cheese Sandwich

A grilled cheese sandwich is a simple and delicious meal made with crispy bread and melted cheese.

Easy, Crispy, and Delicious

This classic grilled cheese sandwich is an easy recipe that is perfect for lunch, dinner, or just a quick snack!

The outside of the bread becomes golden and crispy while the cheese melts inside to create a warm and delicuous sandwich!

Ingredients

You only need a few simple ingredients to make this grilled cheese sandwich.

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of american cheese
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • A small amount of garlic salt

Instructions

Follow these simple steps to make a crispy and cheesy grilled cheese sandwich! :)

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Place one slice of bread butter side down in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the american cheese on top of the bread.
  4. Place the second slice of bread on the top with the buttered side facing up.
  5. Cook for about 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom turns golden brown.
  6. Carefully flip the sandwich and cook the other side until turns golden brown and the cheese is melted.
  7. Remove the sandwich from the skillet, let it cool for a minute, and enjoy! :)

Cooking Tips

Use Medium Heat

Cooking over medium heat helps the bread become golden brown without burning it before the cheese melts.

Choose Good Cheese

American cheese works well, but you can also try Cheddar, mozzarella, or Swiss cheese for a different and unique flavor.

Serving Suggestions

The grilled cheese sandwich can be served alone or with a side dish for a more complete meal.

French fries are a classic choice because their warm, savory flavor pairs perfectly with the crispy sandwich.

Recommended Side

Try serving your grilled cheese with tomato soup, a small salad, or fresh fruit.
